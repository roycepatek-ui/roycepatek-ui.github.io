Night Bloom Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026. Developer: Dexter.

No ads, analytics or account required. We do not collect or send your gameplay, settings or saved shows to developer servers.

Progress, drafts, replays and preferences stay on your device. Reset scores and gallery in Settings. Deleting the app removes local data, subject to device backup settings.

Apple handles optional permanent purchases. Verified entitlements unlock and restore content. We do not receive payment card details. Apple's privacy policy and purchase terms apply to Apple services.
