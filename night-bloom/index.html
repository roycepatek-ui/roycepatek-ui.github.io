Night Bloom

A pixel-art fireworks game for iPhone and iPad. Play to the beat, launch fireworks freely, or arrange a show up to five minutes long.

Support

For questions, purchase help or bug reports, email roycepatek@gmail.com. Please include your device, iOS/iPadOS version, app version, and the steps that caused the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Purchases & restore

The game includes free content and optional permanent purchases. City backdrops include one exclusive rhythm show each. Premium fireworks unlock additional effects. Open Settings → Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account used for your purchase. There are no ads or subscriptions.

Purchases are processed by Apple. Availability and local prices appear in the app. Progress, shows and replays are stored on your device.

Languages

Change the language in Settings. English, Korean, Japanese, French, Spanish, German, Traditional Chinese, Thai, Italian and Hindi are supported.
