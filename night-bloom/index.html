Night Bloom

A pixel-art fireworks game for iPhone, iPad and Android. Play to the beat, launch fireworks freely, or arrange a show up to five minutes long.

Support

For questions, purchase help or bug reports, email roycepatek@gmail.com. Please include your device, operating-system version, app version, and the steps that caused the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.

Purchases & restore

The game includes free content and optional permanent purchases. Paid city backdrops include a dedicated rhythm show each. Premium fireworks unlock additional effects. Open Settings → Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account or Google Play account used for your purchase. Night Bloom offers optional rewarded ads to unlock four Himalayan backgrounds and fireworks, one item per earned reward. You can also unlock this collection with an eligible permanent purchase. There are no automatic interstitial or banner ads, and no subscriptions.

Purchases are processed by Apple on iOS and Google Play on Android. Availability and local prices appear in the app. Progress, shows and replays are stored on your device.

Optional online leaderboards

Version 2.0.0 adds separate song and difficulty leaderboards through Apple Game Center on iOS and Google Play Games on Android. You can play without connecting. If you connect, eligible new completed performances can send scores to the platform under your player profile; local reset does not delete online scores. Manage your profile and available privacy controls with Apple or Google.

2.0.0에서는 곡과 난이도별 온라인 순위표를 선택해서 이용할 수 있습니다. 연결하지 않아도 플레이할 수 있으며, 연결하면 새 완주 점수가 플랫폼의 플레이어 프로필에 등록될 수 있습니다. 로컬 초기화로 온라인 점수는 삭제되지 않습니다.
